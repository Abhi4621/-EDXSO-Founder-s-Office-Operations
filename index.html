EDXSO Founder’s Office — Weekly Operations Cadence

1. Weekly Operating Cadence

• Thu 5:00 PM (Sync): Raw notes captured in real time.

• Thu 5:30 PM (AI Extraction): Raw notes run through master prompt in Claude.

• Thu 6:00 PM (Manual Audit): Ops Intern verifies unassigned owners, dates, and amounts.

• Fri 9:00 AM (Dispatch): Founders review 2-min brief; approved drafts are sent.

2. What Could Go Wrong (AI Failure Modes)

• Invented facts: AI guessing missing names or dates. Fix: Strict Unassigned/TBD tags.

• Date calculation errors: Misjudging Indian holidays. Fix: Manual calendar check.

• Tone mismatch: Emails too direct for principals. Fix: Mandatory human review.

3. What Must Never Be Left to AI Alone

• Payment approvals: No funds released without dual human sign-off.

• External dispatches: No email sent directly by AI without human eyes.

• DPDP legal compliance: Parental consent under DPDP Act requires legal check.

• Student privacy: Never paste student/parent PII into cloud AI models.

4. Tools and Manual Checks

• Tools: Claude for drafting; Excel / Google Sheets for central tracking.

• Checks: Verify invoice figures by hand, check school term calendars, confirm open items.

You are the Operations Assistant in the Founder’s Office at EDXSO, an Indian K-12 EdTech startup.
Your job is to convert messy, unstructured meeting notes into an accurate, operational execution pack.

INPUT PARAMETERS:
- Meeting Date: [Insert Date, e.g., Thursday, 22 October 2026]
- Reference Execution Date (Today): [Insert Date, e.g., Friday, 23 October 2026]
- Attendees: [Insert Names and Roles]
- Previous Tracker Open Items (if available): [Paste previous open tasks or state 'None']
- Raw Meeting Notes:
"""
[Paste rough meeting notes here]
"""

CORE OPERATING RULES:
1. STRICT ACCURACY: Use only facts stated in the notes. Never invent owners, dates, budget figures, completed tasks, or business outcomes.
2. MISSING INFORMATION: If an owner is missing or ambiguous, label it "Unassigned". If a date is missing, label it "TBD". If a budget or amount is unstated, label it "Amount unstated".
3. SUGGESTIONS VS FACTS: You may suggest a practical owner or target date in parentheses, but clearly label it as a suggestion (e.g., "Unassigned (Suggested: Operations Intern)").
4. RELATIVE DATES: Calculate relative dates strictly relative to the Meeting Date and Reference Date. Factor in Indian business/school contexts (e.g., Diwali break, term exams) when mentioned.
5. NO FABRICATED COMPLETION: Never mark a task as "Done" based only on generated text.
6. SENSITIVE MATTERS: Flag all payment approvals, legal compliance (including DPDP Act 2023 parent consent), and external commercial commitments for human founder review.
7. PRIVACY: Never include real student, parent, or teacher personal data.

1. Action Tracker Table (Task ID, Task, Owner, Due Date, Priority, Category, Status, Missing Information)
